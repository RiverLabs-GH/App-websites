# mark.O privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

mark.O is a Markdown workspace that runs on your computer. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your notes.** mark.O reads and writes the Markdown files and folders you open. It never uploads them.
- **Version history, trash and settings.** Saved versions, deleted notes, recent files and preferences are stored in the app's own data folder on your Mac.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign in to.

## Network use

mark.O works fully offline. It includes no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting service, and it makes no network requests of its own. The only exception is content you put in a note yourself: if a note embeds an image from a web address, mark.O loads that image when you view the note, as a browser would. Links you click in a note open in your web browser.

## Assistant (summaries and questions)

The assistant uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on your Mac. The text of the note you ask about is processed on the device by the system's language model. It is not sent to mark.O, to its developer or to any server. The Apple Intelligence feature is provided and controlled by Apple; see Apple's own privacy information for it.

## Children

mark.O collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with the app and the "last updated" date above will change.
